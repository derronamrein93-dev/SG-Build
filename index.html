The Rule of 3

Scan. Sell. Sustain.

Three steps every fitting, every visit—and it repeats. That’s the whole system.

  1. 01

    Scan

    Capture the fitting

    The customer steps onto the platform and stands naturally. Stride Guide captures plantar pressure and load in seconds—no appointment, no training course.

  2. 02

    Sell

    Back the recommendation

    Your associate sees arch profile, balance and hotspots on screen—so the footwear conversation is grounded in something the customer can actually look at.

  3. 03

    Sustain

    Keep the relationship

    The session saves to the customer’s Foot History™, and permission-based follow-up turns one good fitting into a returning customer.

Then it repeats—and every repeat starts with more than the last one did.

Foot History™

One fitting is useful. A history of fittings is intelligence.

Every scan adds another data point to the customer’s record. Over time, a single fitting becomes a trend line your associates can actually use—and a reason for the customer to come back to you instead of a search box.

  1. JUN 2026

    • High Arch
    • Right Forefoot Hotspot
    • Baseline Scan

    First visit. The scan establishes the customer’s starting point and creates the profile everything else attaches to.

  2. SEP 2026

    • Improved Pressure Distribution
    • Second Fitting

    Three months in. The comparison view shows load spreading more evenly—useful context for what to put on their feet next.

  3. MAR 2027

    • Increased Left Heel Loading
    • New Fitting Recommended

    Nine months later, the pattern has shifted. Stride Guide flags the change and gives your team a specific, honest reason to reach out.

Your customer’s fitting history doesn’t reset every time they walk through the door.

Customer Retention

The fitting shouldn’t end at checkout.

Stride Guide keeps the conversation going with permission-based messages tied to the customer’s actual fitting record—not a generic blast list.

  • Messages reference the fitting the customer actually had.
  • Cadence and wording stay under retailer control.
  • Every send is a reason to walk back into your store.

Customer contact information remains controlled and protected. Stride Guide does not sell customer information.

  • Customers control SMS participation.
